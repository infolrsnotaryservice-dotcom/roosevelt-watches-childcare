OUR HEART & OUR WHY

About Roosevelt Watches

Roosevelt Watches Child Care was created in loving memory of Leon Roosevelt Stewart—a smart, loving, caring, ambitious, and goal-driven man who made a lasting difference in his family’s life.

He helped raise and care for me with so much love. Creating a space where I could extend that same care to other children became my way of honoring him and expressing my gratitude.

His legacy lives on through every act of patience, kindness, encouragement, and care. At Roosevelt Watches, we believe every child deserves to feel safe, supported, and loved.

☀️
“He took care of me. Now I get to carry that love forward by caring for others.”
— The heart behind Roosevelt Watches

A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDER

About Me

Hi! I’m Toniyah Garcia, a 24-year-old mother of three boys, full-time college student, and serial entrepreneur with multiple businesses.

Roosevelt Watches Child Care was the first business I opened and my first truly successful business. I have always loved children and dreamed of creating a place where families feel confident leaving their little ones.

My goal is simple: to offer parents peace of mind and give children a safe, comfortable space to learn, grow, play, and be themselves. Caring for children is more than a business to me—it’s personal.
